Idea

{{MARK_DARK}}
Seed
Where a thought starts. White with an indigo core, the same as a mindmap root in the app.
Shoots
Three branches in the product's branch colors, curving out the way rhizo routes mindmap edges.
Sprouts
Smaller growth off the shoots. Ideas keep branching.
Cross-link
The dotted line between two branches. A rhizome has no fixed hierarchy: any point can connect to any other. This one line turns a tree into a graph.
Logo

Lockups and marks

Do Keep clear space

Leave at least the seed's diameter empty on every side of the mark.

Do Respect minimum sizes

Mark: 16 px (use the favicon below 32 px). Lockup: 96 px wide.

Don't Recolor the branches

Use the color, light or one-color mark. Never swap branch hues or add gradients to the wordmark.

Don't Drop the cross-link

Without it the mark is just a tree. It is small on purpose, not optional.

Color

Ink, paper and five branches

The branch colors are the app's own theme palette, so a rhizo mindmap is on-brand by default. Each chip shows the light-theme value on the left and the dark-theme value on the right. Click a chip to copy both.

Type

The wordmark is drawn, not typed

The wordmark is a monoline drawing (x-height 60, stroke 14, round caps), and the tittle on the i is a node. Never set "rhizo" in a font as a logo. In running text it is always lowercase.
